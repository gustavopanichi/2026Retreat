Getting to your return flight

Each SDer is in charge of getting themselves to their return flight. Grab an Uber to the airport, or if you're flying Air Canada you can use their Landline City Shuttle departing from the Palais des Congrès, a 5 minute walk from Hotel Monville, every 15 to 30 minutes.

See the shuttle schedule and book ahead of time.
